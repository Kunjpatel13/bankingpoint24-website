Who We Are

Empowering Local Businesses With Technology

BankingPoint24, operated by Rutva Multiservices LLP, is focused on bringing digital business and essential service solutions together on a single platform. Our goal is to help retailers, agents, entrepreneurs and businesses access multiple services through a convenient and technology-driven ecosystem.

We aim to simplify service delivery, improve business opportunities and provide reliable support to our growing partner network. By aggregating digital payment acceptance (POS, QR), travel, insurance access, retail grocery tools, and educational products, we turn everyday shops into full-service digital centers.

"One Platform. Multiple Services. Better Business."
